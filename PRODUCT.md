# PRODUCT.md — Shreevan Group

## 1. Product Purpose & Register
- **Register**: `brand` (Identity, narrative, and technical export dossier).
- **Core Purpose**: Digital home and official commercial export desk of Shreevan Group, an Indian pharmaceutical merchant exporter supplying finished dosage formulations and Ayurveda products to international markets.
- **Narrative Metaphor**: *Molecule to Manifest* — The journey of an export batch presented as an interactive, inspectable archival dossier.

---

## 2. Target Audience & Users
- **Primary Buyers**:
  - Foreign pharmaceutical importers and regional distributors.
  - Institutional hospital procurement boards.
  - Government health ministries and public tender authorities.
  - International relief agencies, health NGOs, and multilateral organizations.
- **Geographic Corridors**: Africa (East, West, Southern), CIS / Central Asia, South-East Asia / ASEAN, Latin America, and Middle East.
- **User Mindset**: Regulatory-literate, risk-averse, highly scrutiny-driven. They care about COA authenticity, GMP compliance, CTD/ACTD dossier availability, packaging language localization, cold-chain integrity, and reliable Incoterms execution.

---

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Voice**: Precise, regulatory-literate, calm, understated, authoritative.
- **Vocabulary**: Concrete technical nouns (molecules, pharmacopoeia specs, dissolution assays, Incoterms, air waybills, certificates of analysis).
- **Absolute Copy Bans**: Never use `leading`, `world-class`, `cutting-edge`, `revolutionary`, `seamless`, `trusted by`, `best-in-class`, `#1`, or exclamation marks in headings.
- **Anti-References**: SaaS marketing landing pages, tech startup gimmickry, crypto websites, purple gradients, glassmorphism, glowing shadows, floating pill badges, emoji bullet points, stock business handshake photography, fake counters, unbacked testimonial quotes.

---

## 4. Non-Negotiable Honesty Rules
1. Every factual statement originates strictly from `src/config/company.tsx` or is dynamically derived from `src/data/catalogue.json`.
2. Zero invented years, client counts, shipment metrics, awards, or registration numbers.
3. Every numeric fact carries an "as of [date]" footnote.
4. Components lacking verified data render zero placeholders (no "Coming Soon" or dummy text).
5. Allopathic formulations sourced exclusively from licensed GMP-certified manufacturing partners. Never use unbacked "WHO-GMP" or manufacturer claims.
6. Ayurveda products specify dosage forms only, with zero disease or curative therapeutic claims. Destination country classification is noted as the buyer's statutory responsibility.
